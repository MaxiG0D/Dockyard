What’s fixed in Dockyard 1.4.1

Folder icons stay full-size in the Dock

Since macOS 26, the Dock shrinks icons that don’t exactly match Apple’s icon shape and puts them on a gray rounded tile. In 1.4 this could happen to Dockyard folders after the Dock restarted (for example after logging in): the folder’s icon showed up small, inside a gray square.

  • Each folder now draws its own icon in the Dock as soon as the Dock loads it, so it always appears full-size.
  • Folder icons now use Apple’s exact rounded “squircle” corner shape, so they also look right elsewhere in macOS.

Nothing to do on your side: your folders refresh themselves in the Dock a few seconds after the update.
