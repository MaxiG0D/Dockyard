What’s fixed in Dockyard 1.5.1

Apps show their latest icon in folders

Dockyard could keep showing an app’s old icon inside a folder after that app was updated. For example, an app that gained a dark-mode icon still appeared light in Dark Mode until you reopened Dockyard.

  • Dockyard now picks up updated app icons whenever it refreshes your folders: when you switch between Light and Dark Mode, and whenever you edit a folder.

New in 1.5: folder icons support dark mode

If you’re coming from 1.4.1 or earlier:

  • Dockyard folders in the Dock switch to a dark icon along with the rest of macOS, following Icon & widget style in System Settings → Appearance, and switch instantly.
  • Folders that use an app’s icon show that app’s own dark icon.
  • After updating, Dockyard restarts the Dock once so your folders pick this up.
