## What's new in 1.4

### See which apps are open, right in your folders
- Apps that are running now show a small **dot under their icon** when you open a folder, just like the Dock does.
- The dots follow your Dock setting: if you've turned off **Show indicators for open applications** in System Settings → Desktop & Dock, Dockyard hides them too.

### Show, Hide and Quit from a folder
Right-click a running app inside a folder to:
- **Show** it (bring it to the front)
- **Hide** it
- **Quit** it. This works like Quit in the Dock, so apps can still ask you to save your work first.

Apps that aren't running still offer **Open**, plus **Show in Finder** and **Remove from Folder**.

### Fixes
- Dragging an app out of a folder popup now uses the app itself, instead of quietly creating a temporary copy of it.

### Tip
macOS always shows running apps in the Dock, and apps can't hide each other from it. To keep the Dock tidy, turn off **Show suggested and recent apps in Dock** in System Settings → Desktop & Dock. Then apps you've closed won't stay in the Dock.

## Install
Already on 1.3? Dockyard updates itself. Choose **Check for Updates…** in the menu bar menu to get it now.

Otherwise, download **Dockyard-1.4.zip** below, unzip it, move **Dockyard.app** to Applications and open it. Signed and notarized by Apple.

Requires macOS 14 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel
