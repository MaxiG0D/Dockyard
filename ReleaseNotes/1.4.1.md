## What's fixed in 1.4.1

### Folder icons stay full-size in the Dock
Since macOS 26, the Dock shrinks icons that don't exactly match Apple's icon shape and puts them on a gray rounded tile. In 1.4 this could happen to Dockyard folders after the Dock restarted (for example after logging in, or after restarting the Dock yourself): the folder's icon showed up small, inside a gray square.

- Each folder now draws its own icon in the Dock as soon as the Dock loads it, so it always appears full-size, just like before.
- Folder icons now use Apple's exact rounded "squircle" corner shape, so they also look right elsewhere in macOS.

Nothing to do on your side. After updating, your folders refresh themselves in the Dock within a few seconds.

## Install
Already on 1.3 or later? Dockyard updates itself. Choose **Check for Updates…** in the menu bar menu to get it now.

Otherwise, download **Dockyard-1.4.1.zip** below, unzip it, move **Dockyard.app** to Applications and open it. Signed and notarized by Apple.

Requires macOS 14 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel
