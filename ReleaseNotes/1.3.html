What’s new in Dockyard 1.3

Automatic updates

  • Dockyard now checks for new versions in the background, about once a day, and shows what’s new before installing.
  • Check for Updates… is in the menu bar menu.
  • Settings → Appearance → Updates: turn automatic checks on or off, or let Dockyard download and install updates by itself.
  • Every update is signed and verified before it installs.

Also new since 1.1

  • Notification badges on folders in the Dock: a folder’s Dock icon shows the total unread count of the apps inside it, like Messages or Mail.
  • Badges inside folders: apps show their red count when you open a folder; nested folders show their total. Counts update live.
  • Dockyard asks once for Accessibility access to read the counts. Turn badges off in Settings → Appearance → Notifications.
  • New Animation setting: Relaxed, Standard, Fast or Off. Folders also open faster after a click in the Dock.
  • Folder icons in the Dock update right away when you change a folder, without restarting the Dock.
  • Cleans up leftover copies of folders from older versions.
