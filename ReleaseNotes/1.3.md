## What's new in 1.3

### Automatic updates
Dockyard now keeps itself up to date.
- It checks for new versions in the background, about once a day, and shows what's new before installing.
- **Check for Updates…** is in the menu bar menu.
- Settings → Appearance → **Updates**: turn automatic checks on or off, or let Dockyard download and install updates by itself.
- Every update is signed and verified before it installs.

> Coming from 1.0–1.2? Install this version by hand once. From here on, updates arrive automatically.

## Also new since 1.1 (from 1.2)

### Notification badges
- **On folders in the Dock:** a folder's Dock icon shows a real red badge with the total unread count of the apps inside it, just like Messages or Mail.
- **Inside folders:** apps show their own red count on their icon when you open a folder, and nested folders show the total of their apps.
- Badges update live while you read your messages.
- Dockyard asks once for **Accessibility** access, which it needs to read the counts from the Dock. You can turn badges off in Settings → Appearance → Notifications.

### Faster folders
- New **Animation** setting in Settings → Appearance: Relaxed, Standard, **Fast** or Off.
- Folders open faster after a click in the Dock.

### Fixes
- Folder icons in the Dock update right away when you change a folder (color, icon, contents), without restarting the Dock.
- Cleans up leftover copies of folders that older versions could leave behind.
- On the first launch after updating, the Dock restarts once so existing folders pick up the new badges.

## Install
Download **Dockyard-1.3.zip** below, unzip it, move **Dockyard.app** to Applications and open it. Signed and notarized by Apple.

Requires macOS 14 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel
