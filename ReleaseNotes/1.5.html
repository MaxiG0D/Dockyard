What’s new in Dockyard 1.5

Folder icons now support dark mode

Dockyard folders in the Dock now switch to a dark icon along with the rest of macOS.

  • Dark folder icons. In dark mode, a folder’s tile turns charcoal, and the folder’s color moves to its symbol and its Dockyard badge. This matches the way Apple’s own dark icons look.
  • Follows your icon style. On macOS 26 and later, folders follow Icon & widget style in System Settings → Appearance. Dark always shows dark icons. Automatic follows Dark Mode. On earlier versions of macOS, folders follow Dark Mode.
  • Switches instantly. Folder icons change at the same moment as your other Dock icons, including when your appearance switches automatically.
  • Folders that use an app’s icon show that app’s own dark icon, if it has one.
  • Folder previews in Settings and in the menu bar menu use dark icons too.

Good to know

  • After updating, Dockyard restarts the Dock once, so your existing folders pick up dark-mode support. Your Dock items stay where they are.
  • Clear and Tinted icon styles aren’t supported yet. With those styles, folders show their light or dark icon, whichever matches your appearance setting.

Missed a few versions? Since 1.3:

  • 1.4: running apps show a dot under their icon inside folders, and you can right-click them to Show, Hide or Quit.
  • 1.4.1: folder icons stay full-size in the Dock after it restarts.
