What’s new in Dockyard 1.4

See which apps are open, right in your folders

  • Running apps now show a small dot under their icon when you open a folder, just like the Dock.
  • The dots follow your Dock’s Show indicators for open applications setting.

Show, Hide and Quit from a folder

  • Right-click a running app in a folder to Show, Hide or Quit it. Quit works like the Dock’s, so apps can still ask you to save your work.

Fixes

  • Dragging an app out of a folder popup now uses the app itself instead of a temporary copy.

Tip: to keep apps you’ve closed out of the Dock, turn off Show suggested and recent apps in Dock in System Settings → Desktop & Dock.
