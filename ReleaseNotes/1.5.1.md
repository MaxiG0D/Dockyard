## What's fixed in 1.5.1

### Apps show their latest icon in folders
Dockyard could keep showing an app's **old icon** inside a folder after that app was updated or rebuilt. For example, an app that gained a dark-mode icon in an update still appeared with its light icon in Dark Mode, until you quit and reopened Dockyard.

- macOS remembers each app's icon inside Dockyard. Dockyard now makes it forget them whenever it refreshes your folders, so updated icons show up without restarting Dockyard.
- That refresh happens when you switch between Light and Dark Mode, and whenever you edit a folder.

### New in 1.5: folder icons support dark mode
If you're coming from 1.4.1 or earlier:
- Dockyard folders in the Dock switch to a **dark icon** along with the rest of macOS. The tile turns charcoal and the folder's color moves to its symbol and badge.
- They follow **Icon & widget style** in System Settings → Appearance (on earlier macOS versions, Dark Mode) and switch instantly.
- Folders that use an app's icon show that app's own dark icon.
- After updating from 1.4.1 or earlier, Dockyard restarts the Dock **once** so your folders pick this up.

## Install
Already on 1.3 or later? Dockyard updates itself. Choose **Check for Updates…** in the menu bar menu to get it now.

Otherwise, download **Dockyard-1.5.1.zip** below, unzip it, move **Dockyard.app** to Applications and open it. Signed and notarized by Apple.

Requires macOS 14 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel
